# Privacy Policy — Pixiv Toolkit Next

This extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data to the
developer, to Google, or to any third party. Everything it does happens
locally in your browser.

## What the extension accesses, and why

- **Pixiv, Pixiv Fanbox, and Pixiv Comic pages** (`*.pixiv.net`, `*.pximg.net`,
  `*.techorus-cdn.com`, `*.fanbox.cc`) — read directly from the page you are
  viewing, to find illustrations, novels, manga, and Fanbox posts you can
  download. Nothing is sent to these sites beyond the normal requests your
  browser already makes to load and interact with them.
- **Downloads permission** — used to save files (images, zips, converted
  video/animation formats) to a location you choose on your own device.
- **Storage / unlimited storage** — used to save your extension settings and
  a local download history/cache, entirely on your own device (via the
  browser's built-in storage APIs). This data never leaves your browser.
- **declarativeNetRequest** — used only to correct request headers for
  Fanbox API calls made on your behalf, so downloads from Fanbox work
  correctly. It does not inspect, log, or redirect your general browsing.

## Third parties

None. The extension does not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or
any remote server operated by the developer. It communicates only with the
Pixiv/Fanbox domains listed above, using your own browser session, to
fetch the content you explicitly choose to download.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (e.g. a new feature needs a new permission),
this file will be updated and the extension's Chrome Web Store listing will
reflect the new version.
